⚠️ IMPORTANT FOR macOS USERS

This game is not code-signed. macOS will show a security warning when you try 
to open it. This is normal for indie games and the download is completely safe.

🍎 How to open on Mac (takes 30 seconds):

METHOD 1 - Right-Click (Easiest)
1. Right-click (or Control+Click) on ProjectHarvest.app
2. Select "Open" from the menu
3. Click "Open" when warned about unidentified developer
4. Done! The game will launch and remember your choice.

METHOD 2 - Terminal Command
1. Open Terminal
2. Type: cd ~/Downloads
3. Type: xattr -cr ProjectHarvest.app
4. Open the app normally

Full instructions available in MAC_USERS_READ_THIS.txt (included in download)

Why isn't it signed? Apple charges $99/year for developer certificates. As a 
student/portfolio project, Project Harvest is distributed unsigned to keep it 
free for everyone!
